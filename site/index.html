About

I'm Rafa, a Brazilian photographer and filmmaker.

Most of the paid work is documentary and brand film. In between I photograph constantly: buildings, birds, landscapes, sport, and whatever the street offers.

The two feed each other. Waiting for a bird to land is the same patience a documentary asks for, and editing film taught me to read a photograph as one shot out of a sequence.

I live between Brazil and the United States and I travel for work.
